## Identification
This procedure was introduced as part of the organization's standardized operating framework and has been periodically revised to incorporate workflow controls, auditability, escalation requirements, and service-level expectations.

## Scope
This SOP applies to employees, reviewers, approvers, and support teams involved in the **maintenance request processing** process.

## Purpose
To provide a consistent, auditable, and controlled process for completing this activity while ensuring that required information, approvals, exceptions, and supporting records are properly managed.

## Glossary
- **SLA:** Service Level Agreement defining the expected processing timeframe.
- **Requestor:** Person initiating the workflow.
- **Executor:** Person responsible for performing the assigned activity.
- **Incharge:** First-level reviewer responsible for completeness and quality checks.
- **Lead:** Reviewer responsible for business validation and escalations.
- **HOD:** Department head with applicable approval authority.
- **Rework:** Additional work required after a rejection, correction, or returned submission.

## Roles and Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Requestor | Provides accurate information and required supporting documents |
| Executor | Performs the assigned operational activity |
| Incharge | Reviews completeness and correctness |
| Lead | Performs business validation and handles escalations |
| HOD | Provides approval where delegated authority requires it |

## Prerequisites
- Valid request or work item
- Required mandatory fields completed
- Applicable supporting documents
- Appropriate authorization
- Relevant reference records available

## Procedure

### Step 1: Request Submission
The Requestor shall create the workflow record and provide all mandatory information and supporting documentation.

### Step 2: Initial Review
The Incharge shall verify completeness, data accuracy, and compliance with applicable process requirements.

### Step 3: Correction and Rework
If information is incomplete or incorrect, the reviewer shall return the request with a documented rejection or correction reason. The Requestor shall update the required information and resubmit it.

### Step 4: Business Validation
The Lead shall validate the business requirement, supporting evidence, and applicable policy requirements.

### Step 5: Approval
Where approval is required, the workflow shall be routed to the appropriate authority according to organizational delegation rules.

### Step 6: Operational Processing
The assigned Executor shall perform the required activity and record relevant actions, dependencies, and completion evidence.

### Step 7: Exception Handling
Requests affected by external dependencies, missing information, policy exceptions, or operational blockers shall be placed on hold or escalated as appropriate. The reason and responsible dependency shall be recorded.

### Step 8: Completion and Closure
The responsible reviewer shall verify completion and ensure that the workflow record contains the required evidence before closure.

## Process Controls
- Mandatory information must be validated before processing.
- Rejected records must contain a documented reason.
- Corrections and resubmissions must remain visible in the audit history.
- Approval authority must match the applicable organizational rules.
- External dependencies and waiting periods must be recorded.
- SLA breaches require a documented reason or escalation.
- Process exceptions require appropriate authorization.

## SLA
| Activity | Target |
|---|---:|
| Initial review | 4 business hours |
| Correction/resubmission | 1 business day |
| Business validation | 8 business hours |
| Approval | 1 business day |
| Standard processing | 1–3 business days |

## Records and Documentation
The organization shall retain the workflow record, supporting documents, review results, rejection reasons, resubmissions, approvals, dependency records, exception decisions, and audit history according to applicable retention requirements.

## Exceptions
Any deviation from this procedure must be documented with the reason, responsible authority, and approval where required.

## Escalation
Unresolved blockers, repeated rejection cycles, critical SLA breaches, and dependencies outside the responsible team's control shall be escalated to the appropriate Lead or HOD.
