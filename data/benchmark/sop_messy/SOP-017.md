Vendor onboarding – working procedure (SOP-017)

Owner opens the onboarding ticket and assigns it to themselves, then collects the registration form and the
statutory documents. Check PAN, GSTIN and MSME status on the government portals. Two requests go out at
this point: ask Finance (treasury) to validate the bank account, and ask Legal/Compliance for sanctions,
blacklist and conflict-of-interest screening. Both run side by side, not one after the other, so one
DependencyWait covers both while either is outstanding after submission.

Meanwhile assess the vendor's capability and quality (questionnaire, references, certificates), give them a
risk category, and confirm payment terms, the NDA and the code-of-conduct acknowledgement. Draft the vendor
master record and submit.

Sign-off chain, every time: Incharge review → Lead approval → HOD approval and activation. Rejected
onboardings get corrected and resubmitted; a second rejection escalates to the Lead.

High-risk and foreign vendors need extra documents, and an urgent one-off purchase can use a provisional
vendor code – none of these change who approves.

Reject using: Required document missing, Incorrect category (supply or risk category), Amount mismatch
(payment terms/price basis not as negotiated), Wrong quantity (declared capacity doesn't match references).

FPY target 85%.
