Leave applications (HR) — SOP-020

The HR executor creates the leave ticket and assigns it to themselves. They check the leave balance and
whether the employee is eligible for that type of leave, then record the dates (taking out holidays and
weekly offs) and submit. The Incharge approves or rejects and gives a reason. If rejected, the application
is corrected and resubmitted.

Anything longer than 10 days also needs the Lead's approval. Medical leave of more than two days needs a
certificate; negative balance is allowed only as loss of pay.

No external dependency: missing certificates are handled by rejecting, not by putting the ticket on hold.

Reasons: Wrong quantity (days miscounted), Amount mismatch (more days than the balance), Incorrect category
(wrong leave type), Required document missing (medical or other certificate).

First-pass yield target 95%.
