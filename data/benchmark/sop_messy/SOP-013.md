SOP-013 – BOQ review (how we actually do it)

Whoever picks up a BOQ opens the ticket on the platform and puts it in their own name. First pass is the
template checklist: every line needs a description, a unit, a quantity and a rate – if any of that is blank,
stop there. After that comes the real work: compare quantities to the drawings, and compare rates to the
schedule of rates and to the last three prices we paid. Write the variance down line by line. Then write a
short summary of the variance, call out anything non-standard, and submit.

Quite often the site engineering people in Operations have not yet confirmed the drawing revision or the
take-off when you submit. If so, put the ticket on DependencyWait. The Incharge won't look at it until
Operations confirms.

The Incharge then approves or rejects, and a rejection must carry a reason code. If it comes back, fix it and
resubmit. Two rejections means it goes to the Lead to sort out.

Note: big jobs (above 25 lakh, or variance over 10%) and BOQs with more than five non-standard items also need
the Lead to approve. Urgent jobs where site work has stopped can be fast-tracked, but nothing extra is added.

Reasons we reject: quantity doesn't match the drawings/take-off (wrong quantity); arithmetic wrong or a rate
above schedule with no justification (amount mismatch); line mapped to the wrong item code or cost head
(incorrect category); drawings, rate references or quotes not attached (required document missing).

We aim for nine out of ten BOQs to get through without a rejection.
