Subject: Invoice verification – refresher for new joiners (ref SOP-015)

Hi all, quick refresher since we've had a few slips.

When an invoice lands, create the ticket and assign it to yourself. Before anything else check the vendor's
GSTIN and PAN and make sure it isn't a duplicate (same vendor + invoice no. + amount). Then do the three-way
match – invoice vs PO vs GRN, line by line. Work out the GST input credit and the TDS. Knock off any advances
or other deductions. Finally prepare the payment voucher and submit. Remember the person who prepares the
voucher can never approve it.

If Stores (or Operations, for services) hasn't confirmed the GRN or completion certificate yet, mark the
ticket DependencyWait after you submit and wait for them.

Your Incharge verifies and approves or rejects with a reason. If the invoice is outside tolerance (price more
than 2% off, or any quantity variance) or is above 10 lakh, it also needs the Lead. Foreign-currency
invoices just need the rate documented. On a rejection, correct and resubmit; after the second rejection
it escalates to the Lead.

Rejection codes are the usual four – Amount mismatch, Wrong quantity, Incorrect category, Required document
missing.

Target is 90% first-time-right. Thanks!
