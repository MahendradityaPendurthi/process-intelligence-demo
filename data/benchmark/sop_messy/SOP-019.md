Incident review & RCA (SOP-019) — team wiki

1) Open the ticket, assign to yourself.
2) Lock down the incident record and set the severity.
Then gather the evidence: logs, photos, witness statements. Reconstruct the timeline, find the root cause
(5-Why or fishbone), assess the cost, downtime and safety impact, and define CAPA with owners and due dates.
Compile everything into the report and submit.

Two inputs come from outside the team and are worked on at the same time: technical findings from
Maintenance/Engineering, and the safety assessment from HSE. While either is outstanding after submission
the ticket is on DependencyWait.

Approval: Incharge review, then Lead approval. For major incidents, the HOD must also approve. Regulatory
reportable or repeat incidents get flagged but follow the same approvals. If rejected: correct, resubmit;
two rejections → escalate to the Lead.

Rejection reason must be one of Required document missing / Incorrect category / Amount mismatch / Wrong
quantity.

FPY goal: 85%.
