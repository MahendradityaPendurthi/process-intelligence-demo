SOP-021 Employee document verification

Purpose: to provide a consistent, auditable and controlled process while ensuring that required
information, approvals, exceptions and supporting records are properly managed.

The verifier opens the ticket and takes it, then makes sure the checklist for the role is complete and that
every document can be read. Identity and address proofs are verified. Education and employment confirmations
are sent off to the issuing institutions or to the background-verification agency – those confirmations are
the one thing we wait on from outside, so the ticket goes on DependencyWait after submission until they come
back. Names, dates, grade and salary are cross-checked with the offer letter and HRMS, the verification
status is recorded, and the ticket is submitted.

The Incharge approves or rejects with a reason; if any discrepancy was found, the Lead must approve too.
Foreign credentials may need an apostille. Rejections are corrected and resubmitted.

Reason codes: Required document missing (missing/illegible), Incorrect category (filed under wrong type),
Amount mismatch (salary or grade differs from offer), Wrong quantity (experience years miscalculated).

Target: 90% verified without rejection.
