Purchase Requests – procurement desk notes (SOP-014)

Identification: this procedure forms part of the organisation's standardised operating framework and is
periodically revised to incorporate workflow controls, auditability and service-level expectations.

The buyer raises the ticket and self-assigns. Go through the requisition and validate the item
descriptions, quantities, specs and the required-by date. Look up the budget head and cost centre on the
latest budget report. Decide how we'll source it (rate contract, competitive quotes, or single source) and
put a value estimate on it. Write up the PR note and submit.

Budget availability has to be confirmed by Finance's budget control team; until they come back, the ticket
sits in DependencyWait.

Approvals go in this order: the Incharge reviews it, and after that the Lead approves. Above ten lakh rupees
the HOD signs as well. A single-source buy needs a justification attached but no extra approver. Rejected
twice? It escalates to the Lead.

When rejecting use one of: Wrong quantity (doesn't fit the requisition or stock norms), Amount mismatch
(estimate out of line with the rate contract or past prices), Incorrect category (budget head, cost centre or
item category wrong), Required document missing (specs, quotes or the single-source note missing). A
rejected PR gets corrected and resubmitted.

KPIs: SLA compliance 95%; first-pass yield 90%.
