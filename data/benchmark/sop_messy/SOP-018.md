Daily Ops Report – SOP-018

Roles and Responsibilities: Requestor provides information; Executor performs the activity; Incharge reviews
completeness; Lead performs business validation; HOD provides approval where delegated authority requires it.

Every day the executor creates the report ticket and takes it. Pull the production, downtime and manpower
numbers from all sites; reconcile them against plan and calculate the KPIs; log incidents, deviations and
what was done about them; then compile the report and publish it.

Routine reports go out with no approval at all. Only if a KPI breach or a safety event has been flagged does
the Incharge review it and approve or reject. Rejected reports are fixed and republished.

There's no external dependency – if a site is late, publish their numbers as provisional. The report is never
held.

Rejections: Wrong quantity – figures don't reconcile with shift logs. Amount mismatch – KPI calculated wrong.
Incorrect category – downtime/deviation coded to wrong reason. Required document missing – site data or
incident note missing without the provisional flag.

95% should go out right first time.
