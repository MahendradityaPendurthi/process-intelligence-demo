EXPENSE CLAIMS (SOP-016)

Glossary
Incharge – first-level reviewer. Lead – business validation and escalations. HOD – department head with
delegated approval authority. Rework – additional work after a rejection.

How it works
Claims are reimbursed only if they're within policy and backed by receipts. The executor opens the ticket
and assigns it to themself, checks each line against the policy limit for the claimant's grade and the
expense category, matches every line to a receipt, works out what is reimbursable and submits. The Incharge
then approves or rejects (reason code required). Rejected claims are corrected and resubmitted.

Over-limit claims, or anything above ₹50,000, need a justification and go to the Lead too. A lost receipt
under ₹500 can be covered by a signed self-declaration.

There is no external dependency here. Never put an expense ticket on hold – if a receipt or clarification is
missing, reject it as "Required document missing".

Other rejection reasons: amount mismatch, wrong quantity (days/nights/units), incorrect category.

We expect 95% of claims to be approved first time.
