## Assumptions and limitations

- Durations are calendar hours; timestamps are naive, one timezone. Business hours are not modelled.
- Processing vs waiting is **estimated** from who holds the ticket. WIP-adjusted hold is still an upper bound on effort.
- The interval after DependencyWait cannot be split into dependency vs reviewer wait; first-decision comparisons use the full submission-to-decision wait.
- SOP standard times and first-pass yields are illustrative (`quality_targets_source: unconfirmed`); SOP-vs-actual gaps are findings for the SOP owner, not proof of a problem.
- The SOPs took effect after the data period, so all conformance results are retrospective.
- The export has no complexity-driver values (line items, values, severity, ...); SOP estimates use base effort and route conditional approvals from the ticket's approval_count.
- No leave/absence data; persistent employee deviations state this explicitly.
- Baselines are in-sample; all thresholds are demonstration values in `process_intel/config.py`.
